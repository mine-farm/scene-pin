- Give the main-toolbar settings button a gray button surface matching the Play Mode controls.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-15

- Initial Scene Pin package release.
- Pin up to eight scenes beside Unity's Play Mode controls.
- Search, locate, open, reorder, label, and customize pinned scenes.
